Payroll/HCM App v1.1.27 — Release Notes
Release date: 13 September 2026
Starting source: verified v1.1.26 release

SUMMARY
v1.1.27 fixes deduction Save validation and fixed-term contract extension authority, adds Special Responsibility Allowance (Days), and introduces Annual Leave forecast booking with protected forecast-approved leave and resignation recovery.

DEDUCTIONS — HISTORICAL DATE VALIDATION FIX
- Fixed the Save-path bug where adding a new ongoing Union Fees deduction could be blocked by an unchanged historical deduction whose old End Date no longer satisfies today's entry-date window.
- New deductions and deductions whose Start/End Date is edited are still validated against the current date rules.
- Unchanged historical deduction dates remain valid and are not retroactively rejected merely because time has passed.
- A blank End Date remains valid for ongoing Union Fees, including when an ongoing pre-tax super deduction and other historical deductions already exist.

ADDITIONAL EARNINGS
- Added Special Responsibility Allowance (Days).
- Rate is fixed at $20 per entered calendar day.
- The number of days is calculated from the inclusive entered date range.
- The allowance is included as taxable earnings and ordinary-time earnings for the app's employer-super calculation; it does not add service hours for Annual/Personal Leave accrual.

FIXED-TERM CONTRACT EXTENSIONS / JOB DATA
- Job Data is authoritative for fixed-term contract extensions.
- Supported workflow: delete the old Expiry of Fixed Term row, add New Fixed Term Contract, then add the new Expiry of Fixed Term row.
- New Fixed Term Contract does not create a break in service or a new employment segment by itself.
- A saved Expiry of Fixed Term Job Data effective date is exclusive: the employee's contract/last working day is the previous calendar day.
- The latest valid Job Data sequence updates the authoritative fixed-term contract end used by leave validation and employment/payroll checks instead of relying on a stale employee master contractEndDate.
- Example: New Fixed Term Contract effective 01/07/2026 and Expiry of Fixed Term effective 01/01/2028 gives a final contract day of 31/12/2027. Leave in 2026/2027 is accepted; leave on/after 01/01/2028 is rejected.

ANNUAL LEAVE FORECAST BOOKING
- Annual Leave only: after dates are entered, the booking screen calculates and displays the forecast Annual Leave balance at the requested time using current saved information.
- Future forecast considers the committed Annual Leave balance, expected accrual based on effective schedules/current work conditions, already-booked Annual Leave and applicable projected payroll activity through the target period.
- Future Annual Leave can be booked when the forecast balance can support it even if today's committed balance cannot yet support the booking.
- Current/open-pay bookings retain the existing one-scheduled-workday negative Annual Leave rule.
- Forecast-approved bookings are explicitly stored so a later work-schedule/FTE reduction does not automatically invalidate/cancel leave that was valid when approved.
- If later work-condition changes mean the employee no longer earns enough leave, the approved booking may take the Annual Leave balance below the normal one-day negative booking limit when it is actually taken.
- Booking UI displays: “Please note: You may be required to pay back overutilised leave if work conditions change and you resign.”
- If an employee resigns while an outstanding negative Annual Leave balance is attributable to forecast-approved leave, final pay creates a separate Annual Leave Overutilisation Recovery line.
- Recovery is capped to the actual outstanding forecast-approved deficit and does not repeat in later calculations/pay periods.

PRESERVED CONTROLS
- Persisted currentCycleId remains the authoritative open pay; the app does not select a cycle from today's date.
- Original anchor remains PPE 04/06/2026, period 22/05/2026–04/06/2026.
- Retro cut-off remains 03/05/2026.
- Finalisation remains manual.
- Finalised historical payslips remain frozen.
- v1.1.26 7-year/65-day LSL migration and service rules remain preserved.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- This remains client-side demonstration software, not production payroll software.
- The parental Unpaid Extension remains the documented fixed 730-day implementation, which can differ from two calendar years across a leap day.
- Real-browser rendering/interaction could not be completed in this execution environment; automated calculation tests and static/simulated-interface checks are the verified coverage.
- Import/export does not have fixtures for every historical app version.
- Annual Leave forecast uses the app's generated/configured pay-cycle horizon and the saved information available at calculation time; it cannot predict future changes that have not yet been entered.
