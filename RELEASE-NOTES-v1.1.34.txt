Payroll/HCM App v1.1.34 — Release Notes
Release date: 03 October 2026

SUMMARY
v1.1.34 adds Customer Services as a new Department option while preserving all verified v1.1.33 payroll, retro, LSL, leave, casual-employment, login/access, roster, Job Data and finalised-history behaviour.

POSITION DATA — DEPARTMENTS
- Added Customer Services to the Department dropdown in Position Data.
- Existing departments remain available and unchanged.
- Positions assigned to Customer Services preserve that department through normal save/load and migration/import handling.
- Job Data continues to inherit the Department from the selected Position Data record, so Customer Services positions flow through existing employee/job history and report fields without a separate data model.

COMPATIBILITY
- v1.1.34 preserves the v1.1.33 localStorage/import-export data model.
- No employee, payroll, leave, deduction, LSL, Job Data, position, credential or finalised-payslip data is rewritten for this change.

TESTING
- Full automated Node regression suite passes: 156 self-invoking test blocks, 693 assert/assert.* call sites and 71 runtime PASS summaries.
- Customer Services has dedicated regression coverage for Position Data availability and migration preservation.
- data-store.js, payroll-engine.js, app.js and test-cases.js pass node --check.
- Local HTTP serving returned HTTP 200.
- Chromium headless was attempted, but the environment timed out before returning a rendered DOM; no successful real-browser interaction pass is claimed.
- The final packaged ZIP is freshly extracted and the full suite/syntax checks are rerun from that clean copy before release.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- ESS and MSS remain Coming Soon landing destinations.
- Login/password protection remains client-side demonstration functionality rather than production-grade authentication.
- Real-browser interaction testing is reported separately in test-results.txt and is not inferred from syntax/Node tests.
