Payroll/HCM App v1.1.25
Release date: 6 September 2026
Starting version: v1.1.24

RELEASE SUMMARY
v1.1.25 packages the accumulated requests made after v1.1.24. Existing configured pay-cycle selection, manual finalisation, historical payslips, retro rules and import/export structures are preserved.

CHANGES

1. Open-ended Union Fees
- Union Fees remains an amount-only post-tax deduction.
- End Date may be left blank.
- A blank End Date means the deduction remains active in later pay periods until it is end-dated or removed.
- Migration/import continues to clear any stale Union Fees percentage so the deduction remains fixed-amount only.

2. Multiple simultaneous deductions
- Employees may have multiple deductions active at the same time.
- This includes multiple records of the same deduction type, including multiple Union Fees deductions.
- Each active deduction is calculated separately and all applicable amounts are accumulated.
- Concurrent deduction lines remain distinct on the payslip deduction data.

3. Payslip Annual Leave and Personal Leave balances
- Payslip Annual Leave and Personal Leave balances are explicitly regression-protected as balances as at that pay.
- Leave taken in the current pay is deducted in that payslip's balance immediately.
- Example: if 7.5 hours Annual Leave is taken in the current pay, the payslip balance includes a 7.5-hour deduction for that leave.
- Current-pay accruals are also included, so the displayed closing balance is opening/committed balance + current accrual - current-pay leave usage (plus any applicable retro/cash-out/payout effects).
- Re-running Calculate Pay does not reset the balance or repeat the leave deduction.
- Finalisation preserves the same balance snapshot on the finalised payslip and commits that balance once.

PRESERVED v1.1.24 BEHAVIOUR
- Job Data Effective Date remains the first day the new action/status applies; saved terminations are exclusive and automatic fixed-term contractEndDate remains inclusive.
- Annual Leave and Personal Leave may go negative by up to one scheduled workday.
- Payslip date-range filtering still defaults to the 10 most recent payslips and can be widened.
- Union Fees remains post-tax, fixed-amount only and import/export compatible.

DATA / UPGRADE COMPATIBILITY
- State remains stored in localStorage under payrollAppData.
- No employee-data schema replacement was introduced.
- Existing deduction records remain compatible; open-ended deductions use the existing blank endDate representation.
- Existing pay/payslip and leave balance fields are retained.
- Export Data before replacing an existing deployment remains strongly recommended.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- The historical Personal Leave repair remains limited to the detectable prior double-deduction pattern and does not prove every historical balance has been repaired.
- Parental Leave - Unpaid Extension remains implemented as a fixed 730-day maximum; this can differ from two calendar years across a leap day.
- No successful full real-browser workflow/visual regression was completed in this execution environment.
- Import/export compatibility is regression-tested for selected legacy scenarios, not every historical release export.

DEFERRED / NOT INCLUDED
- Casual payments through Additional Earnings.
- Rosters and timesheets.
- Multiple concurrent positions per employee.
