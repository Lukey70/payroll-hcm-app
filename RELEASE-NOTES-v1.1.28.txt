Payroll/HCM App v1.1.28 — Release Notes
Release date: 13 September 2026
Starting source: verified v1.1.27 release

SUMMARY
v1.1.28 adds a Monthly Absence Calendar report, introduces advance fixed-term contract-expiry notifications, and adds controlled recovery/reinstatement when a fixed-term expiry payout was made only because a contract extension was entered late. Genuine Rehire actions are explicitly protected from that recovery logic.

MONTHLY ABSENCE CALENDAR
- Added a separate Monthly Absence Calendar report in Reports.
- Shows all employee records in one monthly grid, one employee per row and one day per column.
- Shows one month at a time with left/right arrow navigation.
- Reuses the exact existing Absence Calendar key/legend, colours and leave-category semantics; the existing Absence Calendar key was not changed.
- Does not include Position or Labor columns.
- Public Holiday, Non Rostered Day, Annual Leave, Personal Leave, Long Service Leave, Leave Without Pay and Other Leave use the same status helper as the existing Absence Calendar.
- Confidential Family and Domestic Violence Leave continues to display as Private/Other Leave rather than revealing the leave type.
- Non-approved leave can show an asterisk/dashed marker where such a status exists.
- Supports browser Print / Save PDF and standalone HTML download.
- Print/download layout is landscape-oriented for the full monthly employee/day grid.

FIXED-TERM CONTRACT EXPIRY NOTIFICATIONS
- When the latest authoritative Job Data fixed-term contract end falls in an upcoming pay period, the expiry alert becomes eligible after the pay close of the immediately previous pay period.
- Notification wording: “[First name] [Last name]’s contract is expiring on [date]. Please complete the exit checklist or extend [First name]’s contract in Job Data.”
- Uses the current authoritative fixed-term contract end reconciled from Job Data.
- Does not create duplicate alerts for the same employee/expiry date.
- If Job Data extends the contract after an unread old expiry alert exists, the obsolete alert is marked read/obsolete rather than continuing to present stale information.
- The alert links to Job Data and preselects the employee.

LATE FIXED-TERM EXTENSION — TERMINATION LEAVE PAYOUT RECOVERY
- If a finalised fixed-term expiry produced termination leave payouts and a late New Fixed Term Contract is subsequently entered effective the next calendar day, the next open pay treats this as a continuous-employment correction.
- The original finalised historical payslip is preserved and is not rewritten.
- The next open pay generates separate negative correction lines for the actual previously paid components:
  - Annual Leave Payout Recovery
  - Long Service Leave Payout Recovery
  - Pro-rata LSL Payout Recovery (only if such a payout actually existed)
- Recovery uses the original paid units and dollar amounts, preventing over-recovery.
- Annual Leave and LSL balances are reinstated by the corresponding recovered units.
- Termination-leave withholding is recalculated as a negative/reversing adjustment under the app’s existing simplified termination-leave tax model rather than treating the recovery as ordinary taxable pay.
- Recovery is keyed to the original finalised payout and occurs once only; recalculation or later pay periods do not duplicate it.

GENUINE REHIRE SAFEGUARD
- Genuine Rehire Job Data does not trigger late fixed-term extension payout recovery.
- Prior termination leave payouts remain final and the previously paid leave is not reinstated for a genuine rehire.
- Existing Personal Leave / LSL break-in-service rules remain applicable to genuine Rehire actions.

CORE PAYROLL/LSL RULES PRESERVED
- Persisted currentCycleId remains the authoritative open pay.
- Original anchor remains PPE 04/06/2026, period 22/05/2026–04/06/2026; pay close 29/05/2026.
- Retro cut-off remains 03/05/2026.
- Finalisation remains manual.
- Finalised historical payslips remain frozen.
- v1.1.26 recurring 7-year / 65-working-day LSL model, migration, service-break rules and notifications remain preserved.
- v1.1.27 Annual Leave forecast booking and forecast-deficit resignation recovery remain preserved.
