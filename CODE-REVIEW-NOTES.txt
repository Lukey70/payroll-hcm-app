Payroll/HCM App v1.1.16 — Code Review Notes
Date: 18 July 2026
Baseline: v1.1.15

Scope
The review covered the new leave, address, rehire, payout and accrual requirements, plus regression checks across payroll calculations, effective dating, retro processing, finalisation, certification, navigation, printing and persistence.

Implemented changes
1. Leave payout history
- Finalised termination leave payouts and leave cash-outs are excluded from automatic later-period retro comparisons.
- A future recovery is created only when the original cash-out transaction is deliberately deleted/amended.

2. Rehire calculation boundary
- Employees now retain separate employment segments before termination and after rehire.
- Current pay and retro processing do not compare the new employment segment with finalised earnings from the previous segment.
- Rehire through Job Data resets the current employment start and applicable LSL service baseline while retaining historical payroll records.

3. Structured, effective-dated addresses
- Address data is split into Address, Town/Suburb, State, Postcode and Country.
- Payslips display: employee full name; address; suburb/state/postcode; country.
- Finalised payslips retain their saved address snapshot; open/future payslips use the address effective for the payment date.
- Legacy free-text address data is migrated into the new Address field.

4. Personal Leave evidence
- The booking form displays “Evidence Provided?” under Absence Duration for Personal Leave only.
- A booking of 3 or more scheduled working days is blocked unless evidence is selected.

5. Overpayment Adjustment
- Overpayment Adjustment is non-ordinary, non-OTE and non-leave-accruing.
- It contributes no ordinary hours, service hours, Annual Leave, Personal Leave or LSL accrual.

6. Bereavement Leave
- Added as a leave type.
- Maximum 5 scheduled working days per booking.
- Displayed under Other Leave on the Absence Calendar.

7. Confidential Family and Domestic Violence Leave
- Models 10 paid days upfront for all employment types, renewing on the current employment anniversary with no carryover or progressive accrual.
- Supports scheduled hours and partial-day bookings within the app’s existing roster/rate model.
- The true leave type and balance remain internal; the booking form shows the confidential available balance for authorised processing.
- Employee-facing calendar display uses Private Leave.
- Payslip rows use the employee’s normal earnings description and do not reveal the leave name or balance.

Quality fixes made during review
- Corrected invalid hidden control characters in Job Data New Hire/Rehire regular expressions.
- Added migration coverage for legacy addresses and rehire histories.
- Added targeted tests for payout suppression, rehire boundaries, confidential leave, evidence validation, non-accruing adjustments and print sizing.

Important limitation
This package is a client-side demonstration, not certified payroll software. Production use requires server-side security, role-based access, privacy protections, complete industrial-instrument rate handling and independent legal/payroll validation.
