Payroll/HCM App v1.1.36 — Code Review Notes

Scope reviewed for this release
- Effective-dated payslip Department and future Job Data isolation.
- Retro Casual Earnings with future contract changes.
- Position Structure Report and As Of occupancy logic.
- Manual Higher Duties Allowance calculation and validation.
- Temporary movement Higher Duties treatment, paid/unpaid leave and return handling.
- One-time FY2026-27 Acting Higher Level reclassification, notification and cumulative retro settlement.

Findings / implemented corrections
1. Payslip position had been made effective-dated in v1.1.35, but Department could still come from an employee master field updated by future Job Data. v1.1.36 stores/renders the effective Department on each calculated payslip.
2. Retro Casual Earnings are calculated from their historical selected position/rate and remain payable even when a future Fixed Term/Permanent contract exists.
3. Position Structure Report uses effective-dated Job Data for occupants/acting assignments and existing Position Data Reports To relationships, with vacancies retained.
4. Higher Duties Allowance uses the employee's latest substantive Permanent pay rate as the normal-rate base, preventing chained temporary acting assignments from incorrectly using the previous acting rate.
5. Acting Higher/Lower Level now separates substantive Regular Pay from the positive/negative differential. Same-level/permanent movements do not generate HDA. Paid leave within acting receives the differential; unpaid leave does not.
6. The one-time Acting Higher Level reclassification is settled through cumulative retro rows and notifications. Existing pre-upgrade Acting Lower/Same Level history is excluded from the one-time historical reclassification, while new presentation applies from the upgrade open cycle.
