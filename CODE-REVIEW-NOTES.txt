Payroll/HCM App v1.1.20 — Code Review Notes

BASELINE
Built directly from Payroll/HCM App v1.1.19 without resetting or replacing stored payroll data.

RETRO SETTLEMENT / OSCILLATION FIX
- Reworked the historical retro comparison to settle all eligible finalised cycles cumulatively within the employee's current employment segment.
- Prior finalised retro payments and retro recoveries are now included once in the cumulative settlement total instead of being re-applied separately to every historical cycle whose date range they overlap.
- This prevents a consolidated or broadly dated retro row from being double-counted across multiple prior cycles.
- Once a positive retro and matching recovery have both finalised, the remaining retro balance is zero and later Calculate Pay runs do not recreate the adjustment unless the underlying historical entitlement changes again.
- Payout/cash-out rows remain excluded from the general retro comparison, preserving the separate leave-payout safeguards introduced previously.
- Rehire/current-employment-segment boundaries remain in force, so prior employment history is not pulled into a new employment segment.

TARGETED REPORTED SCENARIO
A regression test now stores:
1. a finalised +8.29 Regular Pay Retro row with a broad historical date range;
2. a finalised -8.29 Regular Pay Retro recovery with a narrower historical date range; and
3. a later open pay.
The later pay must produce no Regular Pay Retro because the two finalised adjustments settle to zero.

UNCHANGED
- Terminated employees can still receive valid Additional Earnings without Regular Pay after termination.
- Current Annual Leave Loading remains on the same payslip as Annual Leave/Regular Pay for the same position/base rate.
- Casual-payment and concurrent-position workflows remain unchanged as requested.
