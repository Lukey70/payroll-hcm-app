Payroll/HCM App v1.1.28 — Code Review Notes

- Source base: verified v1.1.27 release.
- Calculation logic remains in payroll-engine.js; UI/report orchestration remains in app.js; persistence/migration remains in data-store.js.
- New Monthly Absence Calendar and the existing Absence Calendar share PayrollEngine.absenceCalendarStatus plus one app-level legend helper so colour/type semantics cannot drift independently.
- Contract-expiry alerts use the authoritative Job Data-reconciled contractEndDate and PAY_CYCLES pay-close dates.
- Late fixed-term leave-payout recovery is generated only from finalised historical payout rows whose employee snapshot records Expiry of Fixed Term and whose payout date is immediately followed by a saved New Fixed Term Contract effective date.
- A same-date Rehire row explicitly suppresses recovery.
- Recovery rows carry a stable lateFixedTermRecoveryKey that is retained on the finalised payslip and prevents repeat recovery.
- Leave payout recovery rows are excluded from ordinary earnings and handled in the termination-leave tax component as negative adjustments.
- Annual Leave and LSL usage/reinstatement logic recognises the recovery rows, allowing finalisation to restore the corresponding leave units without rewriting historical payslips.
- Full Node regression suite and syntax checks pass. Headless Chromium DOM rendering remains unavailable due environment timeout and is not claimed as passed.
