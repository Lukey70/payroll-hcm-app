Payroll/HCM App v1.1.22 — Code Review Notes

BASELINE
Built directly from Payroll/HCM App v1.1.21. Existing payroll data remains under the same localStorage key and is migrated in place.

RETRO PERSONAL LEAVE BALANCE FIX
- Root cause identified in consolidatePayslipRetroRows(): the first retro row copied its existing balanceUnits/accrualUnits and then added those values again, doubling the leave-credit movement.
- The consolidation accumulator now starts balanceUnits and accrualUnits at zero before adding each row.
- A one-day retro Personal Leave booking now carries exactly one day of balanceUnits, so Personal Leave is deducted once only.
- Current-period leave accrual remains based on ordinary hours and is not duplicated by the retro replacement pair.

ONE-TIME PERSONAL LEAVE BALANCE REPAIR
- v1.1.22 scans finalised payslips for the prior doubled Personal Leave Retro signature.
- Where a reliable previous finalised balance snapshot exists and there has not been a later manual Absence Balance adjustment/recalculation, the employee's stored Personal Leave balance is rebuilt across the affected finalised cycles.
- The rebuild uses the finalised cycle's Personal Leave accrual, current Personal Leave taken, and the correct displayed retro Personal Leave units rather than the previously doubled balanceUnits.
- The repair runs once, is recorded under state.repairs, and writes a Job Summary/audit record when it changes a balance.
- Employees with a later manual balance adjustment/recalculation are deliberately not overwritten automatically.

LEAVE WITHOUT PAY RETRO PAYSLIP
- Zero-dollar Leave Without Pay changes can now create an informational "Leave Without Pay Retro" row with units and the applicable rate.
- The related Regular Pay Retro recovery/correction remains the financial line; the LWOP Retro line does not add to gross, super or leave accrual.
- The line is retained by payslip consolidation even though its amount is zero.

PARENTAL LEAVE
- Added Parental Leave - Paid, Parental Leave - Unpaid, and Parental Leave - Unpaid Extension.
- Paid Parental Leave supports Full Pay and Half Pay.
- Paid entitlement is tracked as 18 full-pay-equivalent weeks: Full Pay consumes one entitlement day per calendar day; Half Pay consumes one-half day, allowing up to 36 calendar weeks at half pay.
- Standard unpaid parental leave is capped at 34 weeks and reduces as the paid calendar period exceeds 18 weeks; a full 36-week half-pay booking leaves a maximum 16 weeks unpaid.
- Unpaid Extension is capped at 730 calendar days (2 years).
- Maximums are cumulative across bookings.
- Paid parental leave pays scheduled working hours at 100% or 50% of the normal rate. Unpaid types generate no earnings and do not accrue leave.
- Public holidays inside a parental-leave booking do not create a separate public-holiday payment.
- All parental leave types use the existing dark-green Other Leave category on the Absence Calendar.
