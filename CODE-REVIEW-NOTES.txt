Payroll/HCM App v1.1.35 — Code Review Notes

Scope reviewed for this release
- Two-week roster anchor and schedule resolver.
- Leave/Absence Calendar schedule usage.
- Payslip grouping/header position for casual employees with future Job Data.
- Termination leave payout ordering relative to retro calculations.
- Regression compatibility with existing retro settlement, finalisation and leave balance rules.

Findings / implemented corrections
1. v1.1.34 anchored Week 1 to the first seven days of PPE 04/06/2026. This was reversed. v1.1.35 anchors Week 1 / Pay Week to 29/05/2026–04/06/2026 and Week 2 / Pay Close Week to 22/05/2026–28/05/2026, alternating in seven-day blocks.
2. Casual Earnings payslip grouping used the employee master position as a fallback. Saving a future Fixed Term Job Data row can update that master position, causing an earlier casual payslip to show the future position. v1.1.35 derives the payslip header from effective-dated employment/Job Data for the pay period and explicitly shows Casual while casual.
3. Termination Annual Leave Payout was created before current-pay retro rows were appended. A new retro LWOP could therefore reverse historical leave accrual only after the payout had already been calculated. v1.1.35 supplies the complete pre-payout current+retro row set to projected balance calculation before creating termination payouts.
