Payroll/HCM App v1.1.36

DEPLOYMENT
Upload all application files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. v1.1.36 preserves the payrollAppData/localStorage structure and migrates it through DataStore.migrate().

IMPORTANT BEFORE UPDATE
Ensure at least one active employee's current Position Data record has "Access to Payroll Management" enabled. Payroll Management tile visibility continues to follow that effective position flag.

V1.1.36 NOTES
- Payslip Department now uses the effective-dated Job Data assignment; future contract departments no longer bleed backward.
- Historical Casual Earnings retro remains payable when a future non-casual contract is already recorded.
- Added Position Structure Report with As Of date, occupants, vacancies, Reports To, print and HTML download.
- Added Higher Duties Allowance as a manual Additional Earning and as the differential-pay method for temporary acting movements.
- Acting Higher Level uses positive HDA, Acting Lower Level negative HDA, Acting Same Level no HDA, and permanent movements no HDA.
- Paid leave within an acting period continues HDA; unpaid leave does not.
- One-time Acting Higher Level classification correction applies from 01/07/2026, keeps total pay unchanged, leaves historical leave balances unchanged, and creates a notification for each affected employee.

DATA
Existing employees, Job Data, schedules, Position Data, payroll history, finalised payslips, leave, balances, deductions, Additional Earnings, certifications, alerts and login credentials are preserved.
