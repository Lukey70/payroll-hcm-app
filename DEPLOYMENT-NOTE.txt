Raiser replies notify only assignee, or nobody if unassigned. Payroll replies and resolution notify raiser. Reopening requires a reason and is available only while Resolved; Closed requires a new case. Fourteen-day auto closure checks startup/every minute while running.
Attachments: up to five per submission/message, 1 MB each, 2 MB/case total. Browser quotas also apply; failed saves roll back changes.
