Payroll/HCM App v1.1.25

DEPLOYMENT
Upload all application files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. v1.1.25 migrates existing saved data in place and preserves the existing payrollAppData/localStorage structure.

IMPORTANT
- The configured open pay cycle remains authoritative; the app does not choose a new cycle from today's date.
- Finalisation remains manual.
- Finalised historical payslips remain stored separately and must be preserved.

CHANGES
- Union Fees may be left without an End Date and continues until end-dated or removed.
- Multiple deductions may be active concurrently, including multiple records of the same type.
- Payslip Annual Leave and Personal Leave balances include current-pay leave usage and current-pay accruals, and are preserved on finalisation.

TESTING
See test-results.txt and RELEASE-NOTES-v1.1.25.txt for actual automated/simulated/browser results and known limitations.

This remains a client-side payroll demonstration and is not production payroll software.
