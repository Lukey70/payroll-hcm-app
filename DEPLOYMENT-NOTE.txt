Payroll/HCM App v1.1.19

DEPLOYMENT
Upload all files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. Version 1.1.19 migrates existing v1.1.18 data without deleting employees, pay history, leave, deductions, reports or certifications.

CHANGES
- Terminated employees can now receive valid Additional Earnings in the selected/current pay period without receiving Regular Pay after termination.
- Current Annual Leave Loading now stays on the same payslip as the employee's Annual Leave/Regular Pay where the position and base rate match.
- Annual Leave and Annual Leave Loading remain consolidated for payslip display where the applicable rate and position match.
- No casual-payment workflow or concurrent-position workflow has been added; those remain unchanged as requested.

Review payroll results before finalisation. This remains a browser-based demonstration and is not production payroll software.
