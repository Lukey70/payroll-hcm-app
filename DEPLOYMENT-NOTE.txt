Payroll/HCM App v1.1.39

Export Data and retain the JSON backup before updating.
Upload index.html, styles.css, data-store.js, payroll-engine.js, app.js and latest-version.json together. The tests/ directory contains compatibility fixtures and is not used by the running app.

Existing payrollAppData is migrated in place. Employees, Job Data, positions, schedules, leave, deductions, Additional Earnings, credentials and finalised payroll records are retained. Open payslips recalculate on startup. Existing finalised records are not rewritten; their display can consolidate HDA and sort earnings.

This version consolidates HDA, creates one payslip per worked position, orders earnings by Begin Date, and independently settles saved historical Casual Earnings. Current and retro Casual Earnings follow their selected earning position; this supersedes the older single generic Casual payslip grouping.

See RELEASE-NOTES-v1.1.39.txt and test-results.txt for checks and exact-case limitations.
