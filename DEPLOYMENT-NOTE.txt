Payroll/HCM App v1.1.34

DEPLOYMENT
Upload all application files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. v1.1.34 preserves the payrollAppData/localStorage structure and migrates it through DataStore.migrate().

IMPORTANT BEFORE UPDATE
Ensure at least one active employee's current Position Data record has "Access to Payroll Management" enabled. Payroll Management tile visibility continues to follow that effective position flag.

V1.1.34 NOTES
- Added Customer Services as a selectable Department in Position Data.
- Existing departments, positions, Job Data, employees, payroll history and import/export data are preserved unchanged.

DATA
Existing employees, Job Data, schedules, Position Data, payroll history, finalised payslips, leave, balances, deductions, Additional Earnings, certifications, alerts and login credentials are preserved.
