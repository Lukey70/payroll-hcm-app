Payroll/HCM App v1.1.28

DEPLOYMENT
Upload all application files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. v1.1.28 migrates existing saved data in place and preserves the payrollAppData/localStorage structure.

IMPORTANT UPGRADE BEHAVIOUR
- Existing v1.1.26 LSL reconciliation markers/history are preserved; the one-time LSL reconciliation does not rerun because v1.1.28 is opened.
- Existing v1.1.27 Annual Leave forecast approvals and Job Data fixed-term extension history are preserved.
- Finalised historical payslips are not rewritten.
- If saved data contains a finalised fixed-term expiry leave payout and a matching continuous New Fixed Term Contract correction, the recovery appears in the next open pay only; it does not alter the old payslip.
- Genuine Rehire actions never trigger that payout recovery.

MAJOR CHANGES
- Reports: Monthly Absence Calendar with all employees, monthly arrow navigation, existing Absence Calendar key/colours, print/save-PDF and HTML download.
- Notifications: fixed-term contract expiry alert after the pay close of the immediately previous pay period.
- Fixed-term correction: recover erroneous finalised Annual Leave/LSL termination payouts and reinstate leave when a late New Fixed Term Contract proves continuous service.
- Rehire safeguard: genuine Rehire keeps prior termination payouts final.

PAYROLL CONTROLS PRESERVED
- Persisted currentCycleId remains the authoritative open pay; the app does not silently select a pay cycle from today's date.
- Original anchor remains PPE 04/06/2026, period 22/05/2026–04/06/2026.
- Retro cut-off remains 03/05/2026.
- Finalisation remains manual.

TESTING
See test-results.txt and RELEASE-NOTES-v1.1.28.txt for actual automated/static/browser-attempt results and known limitations.

This remains a client-side payroll demonstration and is not production payroll software.
