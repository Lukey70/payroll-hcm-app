DEPLOYMENT
Upload all files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. Version 1.1.18 migrates existing v1.1.17 data without deleting employees, pay history, leave, deductions, reports or certifications.

- Annual Leave and Annual Leave Loading are consolidated on the payslip by earnings type, rate and position instead of appearing once per day.
- Statement of Service branding now uses McDonald's California Franchise.

- Statement wording and contact details now refer to McDonald's California Franchise and HR@mcdonaldscf.com.
