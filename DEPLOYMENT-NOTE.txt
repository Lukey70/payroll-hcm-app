Payroll/HCM App v1.1.35

DEPLOYMENT
Upload all application files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. v1.1.35 preserves the payrollAppData/localStorage structure and migrates it through DataStore.migrate().

IMPORTANT BEFORE UPDATE
Ensure at least one active employee's current Position Data record has "Access to Payroll Management" enabled. Payroll Management tile visibility continues to follow that effective position flag.

V1.1.35 NOTES
- Corrected two-week roster mapping: Week 1 / Pay Week = 29/05/2026–04/06/2026 at the original PPE anchor; Week 2 / Pay Close Week = 22/05/2026–28/05/2026.
- Current casual payslips now show position Casual even when a future Fixed Term position has already been entered in Job Data.
- Termination Annual Leave payout now uses the post-retro leave balance when a current final pay includes retro corrections such as LWOP.

DATA
Existing employees, Job Data, schedules, Position Data, payroll history, finalised payslips, leave, balances, deductions, Additional Earnings, certifications, alerts and login credentials are preserved.
