Payroll/HCM App v1.1.29

DEPLOYMENT
Upload all application files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. v1.1.29 preserves the existing payrollAppData/localStorage schema and migrates saved data through the normal DataStore.migrate() path.

IMPORTANT UPGRADE BEHAVIOUR
- Existing finalised payslips remain unchanged; the new amount-based and Retro PFY rules affect how payslip rows are displayed, not historical stored calculations.
- Existing v1.1.26 LSL reconciliation markers/history are preserved and the one-time LSL reconciliation does not rerun merely because v1.1.29 is opened.
- Existing v1.1.27 forecast-approved Annual Leave bookings remain preserved. On resignation, recovery now covers the full actual outstanding negative Annual Leave balance, not only the forecast-approved component.
- Monthly Absence Calendar uses employment periods to decide whether an employee belongs in each month; no employee records are deleted.

MAJOR CHANGES
- Monthly Absence Calendar: hide terminated employees from the month after termination; re-show from rehire month.
- Payslip: blank Units/Rate for direct-dollar Additional Earnings.
- Payslip: display previous-financial-year retro earnings as Retro PFY.
- Final pay: recover any actual negative Annual Leave balance on resignation, once only.

PAYROLL CONTROLS PRESERVED
- Persisted currentCycleId remains the authoritative open pay.
- Original anchor remains PPE 04/06/2026, period 22/05/2026–04/06/2026.
- Retro cut-off remains 03/05/2026.
- Finalisation remains manual.

TESTING
See test-results.txt and RELEASE-NOTES-v1.1.29.txt for actual automated/static/browser-attempt results and known limitations.

This remains a client-side payroll demonstration and is not production payroll software.
