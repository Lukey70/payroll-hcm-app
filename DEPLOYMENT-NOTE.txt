Payroll/HCM App v1.1.24

DEPLOYMENT
Upload all application files in this folder together. Before replacing an existing version, use Export Data and retain the JSON backup. v1.1.24 migrates existing saved data in place and preserves the existing payrollAppData/localStorage structure.

IMPORTANT
- The configured open pay cycle remains authoritative; the app does not choose a new cycle from today's date.
- Finalisation remains manual.
- Finalised historical payslips remain stored separately and must be preserved.

CHANGES
- Added fixed-amount post-tax Union Fees.
- Corrected saved Job Data termination/movement effective-date boundaries.
- Allowed Annual and Personal Leave to go negative by up to one scheduled workday and preserved those negative balances through finalisation/recalculation.
- Added Payslip From/To date filtering defaulting to the 10 most recent payslips.

TESTING
See test-results.txt and RELEASE-NOTES-v1.1.24.txt for actual automated/simulated/browser results and known limitations.

This remains a client-side payroll demonstration and is not production payroll software.
