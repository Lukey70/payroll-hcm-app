Payroll/HCM App v1.1.24
Release date: 6 September 2026
Starting version: v1.1.23

RELEASE SUMMARY
v1.1.24 packages all changes requested after the v1.1.23 workspace baseline. Existing payroll history, manual finalisation, configured pay-cycle selection, retro cut-off and import/export structures are preserved.

CHANGES

1. Union Fees deduction
- Added Union Fees to Deductions.
- Union Fees is post-tax and fixed-dollar amount only.
- Percentage entry is disabled/rejected.
- Gross pay and PAYG withholding are unchanged; net pay is reduced by the entered amount.
- The deduction appears under Post-Tax Deductions on the payslip.
- Existing start/end-date, staging/save and import/export structures are reused.

2. Job Data effective-date boundaries
- Effective Date is the first day the saved action/status applies.
- Termination effective 07/09/2026 means 06/09/2026 is the last working/payable day.
- Saved Job Data Termination rows are exclusive even when the reason is Expiry of Fixed Term.
- Automatic fixed-term contractEndDate expiry remains different: contractEndDate is the inclusive final contract day.
- Return from Temp Assignment / other movement effective 07/09/2026 takes effect on 07/09/2026; the previous position/rate remains active through 06/09/2026.
- Migration reconstruction of saved Job Data termination rows uses the same exclusive rule.

3. Annual Leave and Personal Leave negative balance allowance
- Annual Leave and Personal Leave may go negative by up to one scheduled workday.
- The limit is derived from the employee's applicable schedule rather than a hard-coded 7.5/7.6/8-hour day.
- Bookings that would exceed the permitted negative limit are rejected.
- Legitimate negative balances are preserved through projected balances, finalisation and balance recalculation rather than being silently clamped to zero.
- The error checker only treats balances beyond the permitted limit as a leave-balance error.

4. Payslip date-range filter
- Added From Date and To Date filters to the Payslip tab.
- Selecting an employee defaults the range/list to the 10 most recent payslips.
- Most Recent 10 restores the default.
- Apply Date Range allows older payslips to be shown or the range to be narrowed.
- Results remain newest first.
- The default range refreshes when pay history advances while the user is still using the default recent-10 view.

DATA / UPGRADE COMPATIBILITY
- State remains stored in localStorage under payrollAppData.
- No employee-data schema replacement was introduced.
- Existing deduction records remain compatible; Union Fees uses existing deduction fields, and migration clears any stale percentage value so Union Fees remains fixed-amount only.
- Existing Annual/Personal Leave numeric fields may now legitimately contain negative values within the permitted limit.
- DataStore migration continues to preserve prior data and now normalises reconstructed saved Job Data termination boundaries to the exclusive effective-date rule.
- Export Data before replacing an existing deployment remains strongly recommended.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- The historical Personal Leave repair remains limited to the detectable prior double-deduction pattern and does not prove all historical balances are repaired.
- Parental Leave - Unpaid Extension remains implemented as a fixed 730-day maximum; this can differ from two calendar years across a leap day.
- No successful full real-browser workflow/visual regression was completed in this execution environment.
- Import/export compatibility is regression-tested for selected legacy scenarios, not every historical release export.

DEFERRED / NOT INCLUDED
- Casual payments through Additional Earnings.
- Rosters and timesheets.
- Multiple concurrent positions per employee.
