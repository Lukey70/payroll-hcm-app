LIMITATIONS
This remains a browser-based demonstration application using client-side storage. Passing automated tests does not certify it for production payroll use. Real deployment requires secure server-side storage, role-based permissions, audit controls, maintained tax/public-holiday/industrial-instrument data, and independent Australian payroll, employment-law and privacy validation. Family and Domestic Violence Leave pay uses the app’s existing roster and earnings-rate model; production implementation would need complete award/agreement loadings, allowances, overtime, incentives and confidentiality controls.
