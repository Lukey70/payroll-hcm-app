LIMITATIONS
This remains a browser-based demonstration application using client-side storage. Automated testing verifies the implemented rules and common workflows but does not certify the app for production payroll use. Production deployment requires secure server-side storage, role-based permissions, maintained tax/public-holiday/industrial-instrument data, privacy controls, and independent payroll, employment-law and information-security validation. Generated Statements of Service must be reviewed before issue and depend on complete employee and employment-history data.
