KNOWN LIMITATIONS / REQUIRED PRODUCTION WORK
- The app is a client-side demonstration using localStorage and a hard-coded demo password. It is not secure for real employee/payroll information and has no server-side authentication, encryption, role permissions or immutable audit storage.
- WA public holidays are embedded only for 2026 and 2027. Later years must be loaded and verified before processing those periods.
- Embedded PAYG/STSL tables and super/tax rules must be independently revalidated whenever legislation or ATO tables change.
- Termination leave tax and LSL calculations remain simplified demo logic and require jurisdiction/industrial-instrument review before real payroll use.
- Recalculate Balances rebuilds from the history stored in the app. It should only be used where the stored history is complete from the intended opening point.
- Bank Details and Super remain placeholder tabs.
