Payroll/HCM App v1.1.29 — Release Notes
Release date: 13 September 2026
Starting source: verified v1.1.28 release

SUMMARY
v1.1.29 refines the Monthly Absence Calendar, improves payslip presentation for amount-based and previous-financial-year retro earnings, and fixes resignation recovery of negative Annual Leave balances.

MONTHLY ABSENCE CALENDAR
- Employees remain visible for the calendar month in which they terminate.
- From the following calendar month they are omitted while not employed.
- A later rehire makes the employee visible again from the month containing the rehire effective date.
- Inclusion is derived from authoritative employment periods/effective-dated Job Data through the existing employment-status logic.
- Existing Monthly Absence Calendar legend, colours, print/download controls and the existing Absence Calendar remain unchanged.

PAYSLIP — AMOUNT-BASED ADDITIONAL EARNINGS
- Additional Earnings entered directly as a dollar amount display only Description and Amount on the payslip earnings row.
- Units and Rate are blank for amount-based rows such as Bonus, Travel Allowance and Reimbursement/Overpayment Adjustment where applicable.
- Unit/rate based earnings such as Additional Hours, Meal Allowance, Special Responsibility Allowance (Days) and Motor Vehicle Allowance retain Units and Rate.
- Historical/current amount-only rows are recognised from row semantics as well as the new amountOnly marker.

PAYSLIP — PREVIOUS FINANCIAL YEAR RETRO
- A retro earning whose source earning period is wholly before the start of the payment date's current Australian financial year displays the payslip description simply as `Retro PFY`.
- Current-financial-year retro keeps its normal description.
- The simplification is display-only: calculation amount, source dates, tax, retro settlement and internal row history remain unchanged.

RESIGNATION — NEGATIVE ANNUAL LEAVE RECOVERY
- If an employee resigns with a negative Annual Leave balance, final pay now recovers the full actual outstanding deficit.
- The rule applies whether the deficit arose from forecast-approved leave or the normal one-scheduled-workday negative Annual Leave facility.
- Recovery is calculated after current-pay accrual and leave usage, so it is capped to the true remaining negative balance.
- The separate `Annual Leave Overutilisation Recovery` line uses the applicable hourly rate.
- Balance accounting brings the recovered deficit back to zero and finalisation commits it once; later pays do not repeat the recovery.
- Existing forecast-approved Annual Leave behaviour remains preserved, but is no longer the only eligible source of a final-pay recovery.

CORE RULES PRESERVED
- Persisted currentCycleId remains the authoritative open pay.
- Original anchor remains PPE 04/06/2026, period 22/05/2026–04/06/2026, payment date 04/06/2026 and pay close 29/05/2026.
- Retro processing cut-off remains 03/05/2026.
- Finalisation remains manual and finalised historical payslips remain frozen.
- v1.1.26 recurring 7-year / 65-working-day LSL rules and migrations remain preserved.
- v1.1.27 Annual Leave forecasting and Job Data contract-extension workflow remain preserved.
- v1.1.28 Monthly Absence Calendar, contract-expiry notifications and late fixed-term payout-recovery/Rehire safeguards remain preserved.

New v1.1.29 regression coverage includes:
- Monthly Absence Calendar visibility in termination month, exclusion from the following month, and reappearance in rehire month;
- amount-based Bonus payslip row with blank Units/Rate and preservation of Units/Rate for days-based earnings;
- `Retro PFY` display for previous-financial-year retro and normal description for current-FY retro;
- resignation with an ordinary negative Annual Leave balance and no forecast-approved leave, including final-day pay/accrual, full deficit recovery, zero balance after recovery/finalisation and no repeat in the next pay;
- the existing forecast-approved resignation recovery regression updated to require recovery of the full actual final deficit rather than the superseded forecast-hours cap.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- This is a client-side payroll demonstration, not production payroll software.
- Real-browser interaction testing is attempted separately and only claimed if it completes successfully.
- The app's termination-leave/negative-earning tax handling remains its existing simplified Australian demonstration model rather than a complete statutory payroll engine for every historical circumstance.
