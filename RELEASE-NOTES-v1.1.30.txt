Payroll/HCM App v1.1.30 — Release Notes
Release date: 18 September 2026
Source: verified v1.1.29 release

SUMMARY
v1.1.30 implements the accumulated requests after v1.1.29: Annual Leave recovery wording, retro amount-based Additional Earnings payslip presentation, leave approval/status controls and termination conflicts, plus future access-control flags in Position Data.

CHANGES

1. Annual Leave recovery label
- Renamed the user-facing final-pay recovery line to `Annual Leave Overuse Recovery`.
- Calculation, balance restoration, tax treatment and once-only recovery behaviour are unchanged.

2. Amount-based Additional Earnings — retro payslip display
- Direct-dollar Additional Earnings such as Bonus and Travel Allowance continue to show Description + Amount only.
- Units and Rate are now also blank when those amount-based earnings are paid retrospectively.
- If the retro is wholly from a previous financial year, the payslip description remains `Retro PFY`, with Units and Rate blank.
- Internal source dates, retro settlement and tax/audit detail remain preserved.

3. Leave status workflow
- Leave booked directly in Payroll Management defaults to `Approved`.
- The Status badge in Leave is clickable and can be changed to:
  - Approved
  - Awaiting Manager Approval
  - Denied
- Awaiting Manager Approval uses yellow status styling.
- Denied uses red status styling.
- Status changes preserve the leave record, dates, type and hours and append status-history/audit information.
- Awaiting Manager Approval and Denied leave are not processed as paid/unpaid leave in payroll calculations until the booking is Approved.
- Denied leave does not block a later replacement booking for the same dates; pending leave continues to reserve/overlap the dates for review.

4. Termination / future leave conflict
- When a Termination Job Data row is saved, leave that falls on or after the termination effective date is identified.
- Because termination effective date is the first non-employed day, leave ending on the employee's last employed day remains unaffected.
- Affected leave is retained and changed to `Awaiting Manager Approval` rather than being deleted.
- A notification identifies the employee and affected leave booking(s).
- Re-saving the same termination does not create a duplicate notification for the same employee/effective date.

5. Position Data access flags
- Added `Access to Manager Self Service` checkbox to Position Data.
- Added `Access to Payroll Management` checkbox to Position Data.
- Both fields are preserved through the existing JSON import/export/migration path.
- Legacy positions default both flags to false.
- These fields are metadata for the future ESS/MSS/role-based access design. v1.1.30 does not yet introduce an MSS/ESS login or enforce role-based access to the current Payroll Management UI.

PRESERVED FUNCTIONALITY
- Configured currentCycleId remains the authoritative open pay; no today-based pay-cycle selection.
- PPE 04/06/2026 anchor, 03/05/2026 retro cut-off and manual finalisation remain unchanged.
- 7-year / 65-day LSL model and existing employee reconciliation remain unchanged.
- Annual Leave forecast and negative-balance recovery remain unchanged except for the display-label rename.
- Fixed-term extension, late extension payout recovery, contract expiry notifications and genuine-Rehire safeguards remain unchanged.
- Monthly Absence Calendar, payslip date filter, Union Fees, Additional Earnings and historical payslip preservation remain unchanged.
