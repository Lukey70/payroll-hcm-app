Payroll/HCM App v1.1.31 — Release Notes
Release date: 3 October 2026
Source: verified v1.1.30 release

SUMMARY
v1.1.31 implements the accumulated requests after v1.1.30: Leave Status dropdown behavior, LSL balance-adjustment and non-contributory-service timing fixes, one-time LWOP retro settlement, current-tax-data STSL retro handling, public-holiday roster enforcement, non-taxable/non-accruing Overpayment Adjustment, clearer retro separation across leave and movements, and a full Casual employment/Casual Earnings model.

CHANGES

1. Leave Status dropdown and payroll/payslip exclusion
- Leave Status is now changed using a dropdown with Approved, Awaiting Manager Approval and Denied.
- Leave booked directly through Payroll Management defaults to Approved.
- Awaiting Manager Approval and Denied leave remains stored for review but is excluded from payroll calculation, leave-balance deductions and payslip leave lines until Approved.
- Status-history/audit information remains preserved.

2. Manual LSL balance adjustments
- Fixed a save-date defect where LSL Accrued or LSL Pro-rata adjustments could appear to do nothing when the real-world date was later than the configured open pay period.
- Canonical LSL balance adjustments are now effective in the current open-pay context.
- Saved manual adjustments survive recalculation/reload and future normal 7-year / 65-day LSL accrual continues rather than being frozen by the adjustment.

3. Leave Without Pay Retro settlement
- Preserved the existing design where Leave Without Pay Retro is a $0 informational row and the related financial recovery appears on Regular Pay Retro.
- Finalised informational LWOP retro units are now counted as settled.
- The same LWOP Retro line no longer reappears in every later pay after the correction has been finalised, unless there is a genuine new historical entitlement change.

4. Public-holiday roster rule
- Public Holiday Paid is generated only when the public holiday falls on a day the employee is rostered/scheduled to work under the applicable effective schedule.
- A public holiday on a non-rostered day does not generate Public Holiday Paid earnings.

5. STSL retro uses current effective Tax Details
- STSL Repayment Retro now depends on the employee's current effective Tax Details in the open pay.
- If current STSL is No, no STSL Repayment Retro is produced even where STSL was enabled in the historical period being corrected.
- Historical tax records and the underlying retro gross/PAYG history remain preserved.

6. Overpayment Adjustment treatment
- Overpayment Adjustment is now a non-taxable balancing earning.
- It does not attract PAYG withholding or STSL/HELP repayment calculations.
- It does not attract superannuation and does not generate Annual Leave, Personal Leave or LSL accrual.
- It remains separately identifiable as Overpayment Adjustment and increases net pay by the adjustment amount.

7. LSL non-contributory service timing
- Future booked non-contributory service no longer pushes the displayed/current LSL entitlement date before that service occurs.
- Qualifying LWOP over 14 calendar days and Unpaid Parental Leave Extension now extend the entitlement date progressively only for non-contributory calendar days that have occurred by the calculation as-of/open-pay date.
- Future cancelled/changed days therefore do not create an early entitlement-date adjustment.

8. Retro Personal Leave and late movement separation
- Retro comparison now keeps incompatible historical position/rate segments separate rather than converting their net dollar difference into artificial fractional units.
- A retrospective Personal Leave day shows the actual scheduled Personal Leave Retro hours once.
- The related Regular Pay reversal for that leave day remains separately identifiable from later Acting Higher Level/movement arrears and recoveries.
- Different historical positions/rates remain separate where required, while cumulative retro settlement and the existing +8.29/-8.29 regression protections are preserved.

9. Casual Earnings
- Added Additional Earnings type Casual Earnings.
- User selects a Position Data position and enters hours worked.
- Payment uses the selected position's applicable effective-dated base hourly rate plus 25% casual loading: hours × base rate × 1.25.
- Payslip description is `Casual Earnings - [Position Name]`.
- Casual Earnings are taxable and attract super under the app's normal super rules, including the casual loading in the superable amount.
- Casual Earnings do not generate Annual Leave, Personal Leave, accrued LSL or pro-rata LSL.
- Selected position, hours, base rate, loading, loaded rate and amount are retained in the saved earning/audit data.
- Casual Earnings are an Additional Earnings payment linked to a position; they do not create a second concurrent Job Data position.

10. Casual employment model
- Casual is treated as an explicit effective-dated Position Class/employment type alongside Permanent and Fixed-Term.
- Casual-only employees do not receive automatic roster/schedule-driven Regular Pay.
- Casual-only employees have no Annual Leave, Personal Leave or LSL accrual and no LSL entitlement date/pro-rata balance.
- Casual-only employees are excluded from the existing Absence Calendar and Monthly Absence Calendar.
- Casual-only employees do not receive Annual Leave/LSL termination payouts.
- If an employee later becomes Permanent or Fixed-Term, ordinary leave accrual and 7-year LSL service begin from the effective date of that non-casual contract; prior casual service is not backdated into LSL.
- Relevant employee/report screens include All / Permanent / Fixed Term / Casual employment-type filtering.
- Casual leave-balance presentation is hidden where those entitlements are not applicable.

PRESERVED FUNCTIONALITY
- Half-pay parental leave remains full scheduled leave units at 50% of the applicable normal rate, with no duplicate Regular Pay for the same hours.
- Persisted currentCycleId remains the authoritative open pay; no today-based cycle selection.
- PPE 04/06/2026 anchor, 03/05/2026 retro cut-off and manual finalisation remain unchanged.
- Historical finalised payslips remain frozen.
- 7-year / 65-day LSL entitlement cycles, break-in-service rules, reconciliation and notifications remain in place.
- Annual Leave forecast, negative-balance recovery, fixed-term extension logic, contract-expiry notifications, Monthly Absence Calendar, deductions and existing Additional Earnings remain preserved.
